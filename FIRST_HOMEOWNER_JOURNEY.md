## 1. Executive Summary & Mission

### 1.1. The Paradigm Shift
Traditional real estate and home maintenance platforms treat onboarding as a sterile setup wizard—a collection of text fields, email verifications, and empty dashboards. 

**Habitat rejects this approach.**

For a homeowner, taking custody of a home is a profound, emotional, and life-defining milestone. Joining Habitat for the first time must not feel like registering for a SaaS utility. It is **Operation First Homeowner Experience**: the initiation of an unbroken, multi-decade digital and physical partnership. The home is not a static property record; it is a living entity with a story, and the onboarding flow is the homeowner's first guided journey through its history, health, and potential.

### 1.2. Operational Scenario
A new homeowner has just completed their first-ever Stratex Core inspection. The physical home has been vetted, scanned, and certified by an on-site structural engineer. From that exact spark, Habitat initiates contact. We design every single interaction from that moment forward, ensuring a seamless, rewarding, and deeply emotional transition into home stewardship.

## 4. Architectural Guardrails & UX Principles

To maintain absolute alignment with Habitat's core design standards (as specified in `design_guidelines.json` and `HOME_HEALTH_LANGUAGE.md`), all onboarding modules must respect the following rules:

* **The House is Always Center Stage:** The user never interacts with empty pages or complex lists during onboarding. The central 3D Twin is visible from Step 2 onward, guiding attention and grounding data spatially.
* **Explain, Never Alarm:** If the initial Stratex inspection discovered weathered shingles or aging systems, do not display "Critical Danger" warnings. Use reassuring, supportive, and educational language that highlights the structural baseline first.
* **Zero Cognitive Overload:** Never present the homeowner with all options at once. Onboarding is a sequential cascade where orbits are revealed naturally, culminating in exactly ONE personalized goal.
* **Respect the Dark Control Room Theme:** All interfaces must adhere strictly to the dark-graphite aesthetic (`#050505` background, `#111113` panels) with sharp 1px borders (`#27272A`) and precise neon teal (`#14F1D9`) and warm orange (`#FF6B00`) glows. Avoid soft rounded bubbles or generic SaaS purple gradients.
* **Traceable Verification:** Every document, measurement, and score must link back to Stratex Core. The homeowner must immediately recognize that their data is not self-reported or estimated, but professionally certified.
