# BUILD READY COMPLETENESS REVIEW
## PROJECT READINESS SCORE AND COMPLETENESS CHECKLIST

This document details the **Build Ready Completeness Review** engine (Task 10) designed to calculate a project's preparedness level prior to contractor publication.

---

## 1. THE READINESS SCORE METHODOLOGY

The Build Ready review performs an algorithmic checklist audit. Missing or unverified critical data points degrade the readiness score.
* **Current Score:** **65 / 100** (Ready for Planning, but Blocks Contractor Publication).

```
   [Score: 100]
     ├── Property Info & Geometry verified (+20)
     ├── Material & Photo Selection (+15)
     ├── Access & Tear-off Assumptions (+15)
     ├── Budget & Timeline Preference (+15)
     ├── Permits & HOA Considerations (+15)
     └── Unverified Decking & Underlayment Condition (-20)  <-- Current Penalty
```

---

## 2. THE PRIORITIZED completeness CHECKLIST

The audit uncovers several items requiring homeowner action or professional onsite verification:

### A. Critical Blockers (Blocks Contractor Publication)

#### 1. Existing Deck & Underlayment Condition
* **Status:** **MISSING / UNKNOWN**
* **Why it matters:** Wood rot or water-damaged sheathing must be replaced before laying shingles. Nailing shingles to rotted wood voids manufacturer warranties.
* **Who can verify:** Certified Inspector / Licensed Contractor.
* **Blocks Publication:** **YES**
* **Contractor can verify on-site:** Yes, during physical attic inspection.

#### 2. Local Permitting & HOA Approvals
* **Status:** **MISSING / UNKNOWN**
* **Why it matters:** Historical districts or HOA guidelines restrict shingle color and material selections. Installing unapproved systems leads to fines or removal orders.
* **Who can verify:** Homeowner / Contractor.
* **Blocks Publication:** **YES**
* **Contractor can verify on-site:** No, must be checked against municipal/HOA databases first.

---

### B. Non-Critical Gaps (Warns but Permitted to Publish)

#### 1. Attic Ventilation Information
* **Status:** **ESTIMATED**
* **Why it matters:** Poor attic ventilation causes heat buildup, increasing cooling loads and buckling shingles.
* **Who can verify:** Roofing Contractor.
* **Blocks Publication:** No.
* **Contractor can verify on-site:** Yes, during attic exhaust vent count.

#### 2. Manufacturer Warranty Records
* **Status:** **HOMEOWNER-REPORTED**
* **Why it matters:** Determining if old shingle failures are covered under existing warranties.
* **Who can verify:** Shingle Manufacturer / Homeowner.
* **Blocks Publication:** No.
* **Contractor can verify on-site:** No.
