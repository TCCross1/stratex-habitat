## CATEGORY 1: STRUCTURAL & VERTICAL ADDITIONS

### 1. Attached Garages
- **Primary Design Targets**: Roof-line matching, foundation mapping, siding alignment, vehicle entry clearance.
- **Complexity**: CRITICAL (Requires structural framing & foundation plans).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: HIGH (Full building permit, setback checks).
- **HOA Impact**: HIGH (Siding/shingle matching, front-facing vehicle garage door limits).
- **Twin Visuals**: Exterior 3D scan alignment, foundation boundary projections, automatic roof-rafter connections.

### 2. Detached Garages
- **Primary Design Targets**: Outbuilding placement, driveway connection point, framing size (single vs. double vehicle bays).
- **Complexity**: HIGH (Detached structural foundation, electric utility routing).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: HIGH (Detached accessory structure setbacks, height caps).
- **HOA Impact**: HIGH (Must match primary residence architectural theme, location restriction).
- **Twin Visuals**: Yard boundary overlay, driveway connector lines, roof alignment matching.

### 3. Room Additions
- **Primary Design Targets**: Internal partition wall teardowns, electrical panel load increases, crawlspace/slab foundation extensions.
- **Complexity**: CRITICAL (Load-bearing wall analysis, roof integration).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: HIGH (Full municipal building and electrical permit, lot coverage limits).
- **HOA Impact**: HIGH (Matching paint, roof, siding, windows).
- **Twin Visuals**: Split-view 3D interior to exterior rendering, wall penetration markers.

### 4. Second-Story Additions
- **Primary Design Targets**: Floor joist load-bearing calculations, staircase footprint mapping, structural foundation verification.
- **Complexity**: CRITICAL (Requires professional structural engineer validation).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: HIGH (Strict height restriction limits, structural engineering sign-off).
- **HOA Impact**: HIGH (Major architectural impact, sightline and privacy concerns for adjacent lots).
- **Twin Visuals**: Structural elevation scans, vertical expansion models with side-by-side height scale guides.

### 5. Pool Houses
- **Primary Design Targets**: Utility plumbing tap lines, auxiliary electrical sub-panels, changing/wet-room drainage.
- **Complexity**: HIGH (Plumbing, drainage, ventilation, electrical).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: HIGH (Wet-structure setbacks, impervious surface lot limits).
- **HOA Impact**: MEDIUM (Outbuilding styling, visibility from public roads).
- **Twin Visuals**: Utility trenching pathways, 3D building envelope projection alongside pool models.

## CATEGORY 2: OUTDOOR STRUCTURES & LEISURE

### 6. Screened Porches
- **Primary Design Targets**: Timber framing connection to main structure, screen mesh density, ceiling fan wiring, door placement.
- **Complexity**: MEDIUM (Framing, screen panels, electrical).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: MEDIUM (Porch footprint setback check, framing permits).
- **HOA Impact**: MEDIUM (Materials and roofing alignment with the house).
- **Twin Visuals**: PBR mesh transparency layers, lighting and ceiling fan wire pathing.

### 7. Four-Season Rooms
- **Primary Design Targets**: Insulated wall assemblies, thermal double/triple pane windows, HVAC mini-split integration.
- **Complexity**: HIGH (HVAC load, insulation, framing, electrical).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: HIGH (Conditioned space building code compliance, insulation checks).
- **HOA Impact**: HIGH (Matches primary architectural envelope, window styles).
- **Twin Visuals**: Thermal efficiency heat maps, interior glass reflections, HVAC duct/conduit pathways.

### 8. Decks
- **Primary Design Targets**: Post footing depth, ledger board fastening to house framing, joist sizing, railing options (composite, wood, metal cable).
- **Complexity**: MEDIUM-HIGH (Structural load, stair stringers).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: MEDIUM (Footing inspections, railing height requirements, setback checks).
- **HOA Impact**: MEDIUM (Material type, stain color, height, railing styling).
- **Twin Visuals**: Footing anchor point indicators, ledger attachment line, dynamic rail height toggles.

### 9. Pergolas
- **Primary Design Targets**: Post anchors, header spans, louver spacing, material choices (cedar, vinyl, aluminum).
- **Complexity**: MEDIUM (Post bracing, leveling, overhead structure).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: LOW-MEDIUM (Utility line clearances, footprint constraints).
- **HOA Impact**: MEDIUM (Stain/paint colors, placement in rear vs. side yard).
- **Twin Visuals**: Overhead sun/shade simulation based on geo-coordinates and time-of-day.

### 10. Gazebos
- **Primary Design Targets**: Octagonal or round roofing structures, built-in benches, hurricane tie-down anchor plates.
- **Complexity**: MEDIUM-HIGH (Complex roof geometry, anchors).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: MEDIUM (Wind-load requirements, setback boundaries).
- **HOA Impact**: MEDIUM (Roofing material matching, outbuilding placement).
- **Twin Visuals**: Multi-sided framing models, 3D roofing shingle previews.

### 11. Outdoor Kitchens
- **Primary Design Targets**: Natural gas/propane plumbing, outdoor-rated refrigerators, stone/brick framing, weather-resistant stone countertops.
- **Complexity**: HIGH (Plumbing, gas safety, electrical).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: MEDIUM (Gas line safety testing, electrical sub-panel inspections).
- **HOA Impact**: LOW-MEDIUM (Location restrictions, height of vent hoods).
- **Twin Visuals**: Gas line layout, island stone veneer swatches, appliance mockups.

### 12. Fire Pits
- **Primary Design Targets**: Gas lines vs. wood-burning clearances, safe distance from overhanging trees and structures, stone surround.
- **Complexity**: LOW-MEDIUM (Gas connection, non-combustible foundation).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: LOW (Fire safety clearance distances).
- **HOA Impact**: LOW (Rear-yard only, aesthetic material restrictions).
- **Twin Visuals**: Safe clearance radius circles, realistic fire glow lighting presets on the twin.

## CATEGORY 3: WATER & RECREATION

### 13. Swimming Pools
- **Primary Design Targets**: Excavation boundaries, concrete shotcrete/vinyl/fiberglass shell, pool pump/filter pad location, safety fencing, main drain plumbing.
- **Complexity**: CRITICAL (Excavation, water-tight shell, hydraulic engineering, electrical).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: HIGH (Water-barrier fencing laws, electrical grounding, GIS setback surveys, impervious lot coverage).
- **HOA Impact**: HIGH (Strict safety fences, equipment noise screens, landscaping requirements).
- **Twin Visuals**: 3D yard excavation simulator, water transparency and depth shaders, security fencing overlays.

## CATEGORY 4: HARDSCAPE & CONCRETE

### 14. Fences
- **Primary Design Targets**: Post spacing, gate widths, property line alignments, material types (wood, aluminum, vinyl, chain-link).
- **Complexity**: LOW-MEDIUM (Bracing, leveling on sloped terrain).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: LOW-MEDIUM (Height caps, front yard setback rules, "good side facing out" laws).
- **HOA Impact**: HIGH (Approved colors, material bans, height limits).
- **Twin Visuals**: Property line snap-to-boundary lines, terrain-hugging stepped/sloped fence profiles.

### 15. Driveways
- **Primary Design Targets**: Vehicle turning radius, grade slope limits, water drainage swales, concrete slab thickness.
- **Complexity**: MEDIUM-HIGH (Grade/leveling, concrete pour, rebar).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: MEDIUM (Curb cut approvals, right-of-way permits, stormwater runoff compliance).
- **HOA Impact**: MEDIUM (Material type e.g. concrete vs asphalt, placement limits).
- **Twin Visuals**: Drainage flow heatmaps, vehicle approach path simulation.

### 16. Driveway Expansions
- **Primary Design Targets**: Expansion joints, matching existing concrete color/finish, parking footprint enlargement.
- **Complexity**: MEDIUM (Excavation, base compaction, joining seams).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: MEDIUM (Stormwater runoff limits, maximum width rules).
- **HOA Impact**: MEDIUM (No parking on unpaved surfaces, aesthetic match of new vs old).
- **Twin Visuals**: Cold joint seams, expansion layout overlays, material weathering sliders.

### 17. Sidewalks
- **Primary Design Targets**: Concrete control joints, municipal sidewalk connections, ADA slope compatibility.
- **Complexity**: LOW-MEDIUM (Leveling, forms, pouring).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: LOW-MEDIUM (Municipal right-of-way easement requirements).
- **HOA Impact**: LOW (Must match neighborhood standard sidewalks).
- **Twin Visuals**: Pedestrian route line, ADA slope warning overlays.

### 18. Walkways
- **Primary Design Targets**: Stepping stone pathing, brick pavers, flagstone on gravel, path lighting connections.
- **Complexity**: LOW-MEDIUM (Compaction, pattern layouts).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: LOW (Rarely requires municipal permits unless on easements).
- **HOA Impact**: LOW-MEDIUM (Aesthetic paver color limits).
- **Twin Visuals**: Custom paver pattern generation tool, pathing alignment curves.

### 19. Patios
- **Primary Design Targets**: Pavers or stone tiles, drainage pitch (1/4 inch drop per foot away from home foundation), base layers.
- **Complexity**: MEDIUM (Grading, base stabilization, sand leveling).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: LOW-MEDIUM (Lot coverage percentage caps).
- **HOA Impact**: MEDIUM (Rear yard limits, approved stone/paver shades).
- **Twin Visuals**: Sub-base compaction visualization, slope direction indicators.

### 20. Concrete Pads
- **Primary Design Targets**: Flat surface for trash cans, HVAC equipment, shed foundation, sub-base gravel.
- **Complexity**: LOW-MEDIUM (Forming, rebar, finishing).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: LOW-MEDIUM (Accessory pad setbacks).
- **HOA Impact**: LOW (Screes/screening requirements for visual blocks of trash cans/HVAC).
- **Twin Visuals**: Equipment footprint mockups, gravel base depth levels.

### 21. Decorative Concrete
- **Primary Design Targets**: Acid staining, exposed aggregates, integral color pigments.
- **Complexity**: MEDIUM-HIGH (Chemical stains, specialized finishing).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: LOW (Rarely regulated at municipal level).
- **HOA Impact**: MEDIUM (No overly reflective/bright colored driveways/patios).
- **Twin Visuals**: Stain color blending swatches, aggregate exposure shaders.

### 22. Stamped Concrete
- **Primary Design Targets**: Pattern mats (ashlar slate, cobblestone, brick), release agents, antiquing washes, sealers.
- **Complexity**: HIGH (Timing-critical texturing).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: LOW (Aesthetic surface finish only).
- **HOA Impact**: MEDIUM (Approved natural stone/brick stamp patterns).
- **Twin Visuals**: Realistic 3D normal-map texture overlays showing depth on stamped surfaces.

### 23. Retaining Walls
- **Primary Design Targets**: Geogrid reinforcement, hydrostatic pressure drainage weeping pipes, gravity vs cantilever walls.
- **Complexity**: HIGH (Hydraulic loads, structural safety).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: HIGH (Walls over 4 feet require structural engineer stamp).
- **HOA Impact**: MEDIUM-HIGH (Visible wall material match with stone cladding rules).
- **Twin Visuals**: Earth pressure force vectors, weeping pipe indicators, drainage gravel maps.

## CATEGORY 5: LANDSCAPE, LIGHTING, & BOUNDARIES

### 24. Landscaping
- **Primary Design Targets**: Soil preparation, plant selection (zones, native vs. non-native), mulch beds, irrigation zones.
- **Complexity**: MEDIUM (Soil prep, plant health design, irrigation pathing).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: LOW (Irrigation backflow preventer check).
- **HOA Impact**: HIGH (Minimum plant count, turf percentage rules, weed control).
- **Twin Visuals**: Planting bed zones, botanical models with plant growth timeline sliders (1, 5, 10 years).

### 25. Exterior Lighting
- **Primary Design Targets**: Accent up-lighting, path down-lighting, transformer size, low-voltage wiring pathways.
- **Complexity**: LOW-MEDIUM (Low-voltage connections, wattage balancing).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: LOW (Safe voltage checks).
- **HOA Impact**: MEDIUM (Dark-sky compliance, zero light pollution spilling to neighbors).
- **Twin Visuals**: Low-voltage electrical diagrams, realistic night lighting simulators with light cone projections.

## CATEGORY 6: ENVELOPE, UTILITIES, & ACCESSIBILITY

### 26. Roofing
- **Primary Design Targets**: Architectural asphalt shingles, standing seam metal, clay tiles, drip edge, ridge venting, flashing.
- **Complexity**: HIGH (Water tightness, working at heights, wind ratings).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: MEDIUM (Roofing tear-off and deck inspections).
- **HOA Impact**: HIGH (Strict color and shingle material approvals).
- **Twin Visuals**: High-resolution shingle normal maps, valley and flashing detail callouts.

### 27. Windows
- **Primary Design Targets**: Window style (double-hung, casement, awning), U-factor insulation ratings, casing style.
- **Complexity**: MEDIUM (Sash leveling, thermal sealing).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: LOW-MEDIUM (Egress window requirements for bedrooms).
- **HOA Impact**: HIGH (Grid style restrictions, frame color matches).
- **Twin Visuals**: Window frame profile editor, thermal insulation values.

### 28. Doors
- **Primary Design Targets**: Main entry doors, sidelight packages, hardware security locks, weather stripping.
- **Complexity**: LOW-MEDIUM (Plumb jambs, weather seal).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: LOW (Fire code egress widths).
- **HOA Impact**: HIGH (Entry door color palettes, hardware finish constraints).
- **Twin Visuals**: 3D door swing radius, wood/fiberglass grain finishes.

### 29. Siding
- **Primary Design Targets**: Fiber cement lap siding, cedar shakes, board and batten, house wrap vapor barrier, flashing.
- **Complexity**: HIGH (Waterproofing, thermal expansion gaps, working at heights).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: MEDIUM (Building envelope energy inspection).
- **HOA Impact**: HIGH (Approved siding brands, colors, texture rules).
- **Twin Visuals**: Siding overlapping profile models, vapor-barrier wrapping maps, shadow line projections.

### 30. Solar Systems
- **Primary Design Targets**: Monocrystalline panel orientation, inverter mounting location, solar racking, electrical panel tie-ins.
- **Complexity**: HIGH (Roof loads, high-voltage electrical, grid interconnect).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: HIGH (Structural roof engineering check, electrical permits, utility interconnect contract).
- **HOA Impact**: HIGH (Visibility constraints, height limits, screen wraps).
- **Twin Visuals**: Roof solar path mapping, yearly energy generation projections based on local solar insulation metrics.

### 31. EV Charging
- **Primary Design Targets**: Level 2 NEMA 14-50 outlets or hardwired wall chargers, conduit pathing, electrical panel load check.
- **Complexity**: MEDIUM-HIGH (High-voltage wiring, electrical capacity).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: MEDIUM (Electrical safety inspection, permit check).
- **HOA Impact**: LOW (Must keep wiring/conduits tidy and painted to match siding).
- **Twin Visuals**: Conduit routing paths on the house, vehicle docking charging reach radiuses.

### 32. Accessibility Improvements
- **Primary Design Targets**: ADA ramps (1:12 maximum slope), entry door widening, step-free threshold entries, safety handrails.
- **Complexity**: MEDIUM-HIGH (ADA compliance, structural wood/concrete forms).
- **Zoning & Permit Level**: MEDIUM (ADA accessibility safety permits).
- **HOA Impact**: MEDIUM (Aesthetic ramp integration, paint-matching rails).
- **Twin Visuals**: Accessibility clearance envelopes, ramp slope/grade safety warning systems (Green = Safe, Red = Too Steep).
