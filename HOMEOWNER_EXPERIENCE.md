## 1. Core Philosophy: "This Is My Home"

Traditional software makes the user feel like an operator navigating a database. In contrast, Habitat is designed so that the homeowner immediately feels:
> *"This is MY home."*

To achieve this emotional connection, the interface removes sterile grid-containers, dense administrative toolbars, and standard business dashboard panels. The home is not a static 2D JPEG or a sub-page icon; it is the **living core of the interface**. Every visual asset, data stream, and task is spatially anchored directly to the 3D Digital Twin of the home.

### 2.1. The 9 Orbitals
Each orbit is interactive, allowing the homeowner to zoom into specific subsystems on the central 3D Twin:

1. **Digital Twin (Core):** A high-fidelity, real-time 3D model that rotates on a subtle slow-orbit axis. Toggling an orbital layer changes the twin's active rendering (e.g., selecting *Maintenance* fades the structural facade to expose the plumbing and HVAC runs; selecting *Savings* highlights thermal efficiency and solar capture regions).
2. **Health Orbit:** Floats directly above the roof of the 3D twin, showing structural, thermal, moisture, and mechanical health indices using organic, comforting feedback.
3. **Timeline Orbit:** Anchored beneath the home's foundation layer, tracing the home's history like a series of sediment layers, showing chronological events.
4. **Projects Orbit:** Positioned to the left, grouping design packages, material swaps, and active renovation bids. Clicking a project applies the proposed visual layer onto the central 3D twin.
5. **Documents Orbit:** Positioned on the lower-left, holding deeds, building permits, product warranties, and original architectural plans.
6. **Maintenance Orbit:** Positioned on the upper-right, highlighting upcoming and completed preventative tasks.
7. **Savings Orbit:** Located on the lower-right, visualizing real-time energy production (solar), weatherization savings, and local tax credits.
8. **Contractors Orbit:** Floating to the right of the lawn, showing active bids and trusted local specialists assigned to active projects.
9. **Passport History Orbit:** Encircles the entire home like a protective ring, showcasing the transferrable pedigree of structural and physical work verified by Stratex Core.

### 3.1. Question 1: "How is my home?"
* **UX Strategy:** Answered instantly upon opening the application through the **Home Comfort Index (HCI)**. This is not a technical dashboard rating (like *"94% mechanical reliability"*), but an organic, whole-home state indicator.
* **Visual Representation:** The central 3D twin glows with a warm, steady, soft amber-gold aura when all systems are healthy, accompanied by a quiet rustling of leaves on the virtual property trees.
* **Textual Response:** *"Your home is quiet, dry, and performing beautifully today."*

### 3.2. Question 2: "What changed?"
* **UX Strategy:** Answered through the **Living Timeline Feed**. It highlights recent physical and environmental shifts so the homeowner is never caught off guard.
* **Visual Representation:** A gentle pulse wave ripples across the affected section of the 3D twin (e.g., if a new warranty was uploaded for the water heater, the mechanical closet in the basement glows softly with a white outline).
* **Textual Response:** *"Stratex Core verified your new 10-year warranty for the hybrid water heater. Your protective coverage has expanded."*

### 3.3. Question 3: "What needs attention?"
* **UX Strategy:** Displays soft, prioritized, actionable tasks, avoiding alarmist language or red warning colors.
* **Visual Representation:** A soft, glowing, non-blinking teal beacon indicates the system needing care on the 3D model. No aggressive red exclamation points or flashing warning icons.
* **Textual Response:** *"Your HVAC filters have performed their season of air cleaning. Swapping them this week will keep your indoor air exceptionally fresh."*

### 3.4. Question 4: "What opportunities exist?"
* **UX Strategy:** Identifies proactive enhancements to reduce energy waste, lower insurance premiums, or extend mechanical lifespans.
* **Visual Representation:** The savings orbit lights up with a soft green outline, pointing to the specific layer of the 3D twin (e.g., roof sheathing or window frames).
* **Textual Response:** *"Upgrading your attic insulation this weekend would reduce thermal loss by 14% and lower your summer cooling investments."*

### 3.5. Question 5: "What can I build next?"
* **UX Strategy:** Encourages pride in homeownership and stimulates imagination by suggesting realistic, code-compliant, and architecturally harmonious projects.
* **Visual Representation:** Clicking this orbital reveals semi-transparent, ghostly architectural mockups overlaying the central 3D twin (e.g., showing a future cedar pergola or solar roof array in elegant dashed lines).
* **Textual Response:** *"Based on your home's craftsman architecture, a natural cedar deck addition would perfectly complement your outdoor space. Tap to explore curated, code-approved styles."*
