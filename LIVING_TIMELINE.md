## 1. Introduction: Every Home Has a Story

A house is not a collection of isolated repair bills. It is a living sanctuary that shelters lives, survives storms, grows through projects, and ages gracefully over decades. 

**The Living Timeline** is Habitat's core historical engine. It reframes documentation from a dry administrative file folder into an active, chronological narrative. By aggregating inspections, repairs, projects, warranties, storms, insurance claims, photos, family milestones, and property transfers into a single unified ledger, Habitat builds a rich biography of the home. This biography does not just tell you when the roof was replaced; it illustrates how the home has been protected, nurtured, and loved.

### 2.1. Structural Layout & Navigation
* **Vertical Scrolling:** Scrolling downward moves the view deeper into the home's history. The 3D Twin above changes its visual state to match the selected historical point (e.g., scrolling to 2021 renders the home in its original cream paint before the 2026 siding overhaul).
* **Filtering Orbits:** Homeowners can filter the timeline by event type (e.g., viewing only *Warranties & Manuals* or filtering to show how the home performed during past *Storms*).
* **The Verification Seal:** Events verified by Stratex Core are marked with a glowing, immutable security seal, certifying to future buyers that the work was done to professional specifications.

## 3. The Unified Event Schema

To combine technical, environmental, and personal events into a single, cohesive feed, every timeline entry conforms to the following structural schema:

| Event Type | Visual Icon / Style | Primary Core Data Captured | Connected Twin Asset |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Inspections** | Glowing Teal Microscope | Inspector credentials, structural ratings, Stratex Core reports. | Attic, Foundation, or Entire Envelope |
| **Repairs** | Amber Wrench | Issue diagnosed, materials utilized, labor details, cost. | Plumbing Line, HVAC, or Specific Wall |
| **Projects** | Gold Blueprint | Before/after renderings, contractor profile, permit numbers. | Siding, Deck, Solar Array |
| **Warranties** | Green Protective Shield | Manufacturer certificate, coverage duration, transferability terms. | Shingles, Heat Pump, Windows |
| **Storms** | Blue Wave / Wind | Wind speeds, precipitation, localized damage assessment logs. | Roof, Siding, Gutters |
| **Insurance Claims** | Violet Document | Claim ID, adjustor notes, payout amounts, repair receipts. | Chimney, Basement, or Roof |
| **Photos** | Silver Camera | High-resolution before/after scans, thermal imaging drafts. | Target Facade or Mechanical Room |
| **Family Milestones** | Rose Heart (Optional) | Move-in dates, home expansions, holiday lighting archives. | Whole Property |
| **Ownership Transfers** | Platinum Key | Deed registration, transfer logs, historical escrow value. | Property Record |

## 4. Operational Scenario: "A Storm & A Repair"

The Living Timeline is not a passive archive; it actively links environmental events to physical outcomes. 

### Scenario: Severe Winter Freeze
1. **The Storm Event:**
   * *Data Captured:* Temperature drops to 12°F and remains there for 72 hours.
   * *Timeline Entry:* *"Severe Winter Freeze Event. Lowest temperature recorded: 12°F."*
2. **The Sensor / Human Diagnostic:**
   * *Data Captured:* Homeowner notices slow water flow in the basement guest bathroom.
   * *Timeline Entry:* *"Slow basement water flow detected. Potential pipe frost hazard."*
3. **The Repair Event:**
   * *Data Captured:* Contractor repairs a pinhole leak behind drywall and adds upgraded insulation.
   * *Timeline Entry:* *"Drywall opened. Pinhole water line repair completed by Horizon Plumbing. Installed upgraded R-15 foam insulation sleeve."*
4. **The Warranty Attachment:**
   * *Data Captured:* 1-year labor warranty from Horizon Plumbing.
   * *Timeline Entry:* *"1-Year Labor Warranty active for basement water line repair."*

By connecting these four events sequentially on the timeline, the home's narrative moves from a series of stressful emergencies to a documented history of continuous improvement and proactive protection.

## 5. The Ultimate Value: The Digital Passport Transfer

When it comes time to sell the home, the Living Timeline becomes the home's ultimate asset. Rather than hand over an unorganized shoe box of old receipts, the homeowner transfers the **Verified Digital Passport**.

Prospective buyers can browse the complete, certified history of the house, verifying that:
* The roof has survived three major storms with zero verified moisture intrusion.
* The HVAC has been serviced every autumn without fail.
* All major renovations were completed by top-rated, vetted contractors using high-durability, warrantied materials.

This verified narrative transforms trust from an unknown variable into an asset, raising the home's market value and simplifying the transaction.
