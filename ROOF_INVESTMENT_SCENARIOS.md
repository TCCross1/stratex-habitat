## 4. STRICT REGULATORY DISCLOSURES

To maintain absolute transparency and fiduciary duty, the system includes a dedicated **"Unpromised Disclosures"** section:
* **Resale Return:** We do not claim or promise a specific percentage of resale return (e.g., "increases resale by 70%"). Resale returns are volatile and subject to hyper-local real estate markets.
* **Energy Savings:** Thermal reflections are modeled, but we do not guarantee specific utility bill savings (e.g., "saves $400/yr").
* **Insurance Savings:** Lowering premium rates is subject to specific underwriter approval; no discount is promised.
* **Service Life:** Remaining service life is probabilistic based on weather exposure, never guaranteed as an absolute number.
* **Bundling Assumptions:** Any multi-project bundling discount (e.g., combining roofing and solar) is shown as a **Configurable Assumption** (default: 8% potential labor discount), never a guaranteed discount.
