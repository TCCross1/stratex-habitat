## 1. Core Paradigm Shift: From "Leads" to "Scopes"

Traditional residential contracting platforms treat contractors as buyers of commoditized, anonymous consumer contact details (leads). These models result in:
* **The "Race to the Bottom":** Contractors are forced to pay for unqualified leads, competing blindly against multiple firms on price alone, without detailed project parameters.
* **Friction and Overhead:** Contractors waste hours traveling to sites, taking manual measurements, and dealing with homeowners who do not understand material costs or building restrictions.

**Habitat completely rejects the "Lead Generation" model.**

On Habitat, contractors do not buy anonymous leads. Instead, they are granted access to highly detailed, pre-vetted, and structured **Project Opportunities**. The platform provides clear scopes, visual designs, verified dimensions, and direct access to the home's Digital Twin. This shifts the relationship from transactional sales to high-fidelity planning and execution.

## 2. What Contractors Receive

Every Opportunity Package is a comprehensive, self-contained project dossier containing everything needed to formulate an accurate, binding bid.

### 2.1. Well-Defined Opportunities
Contractors receive a complete technical breakdown of the requested work. For example, instead of a simple request for *"siding repair"*, contractors receive:
> *"Complete siding replacement of 2,400 sq. ft. of exterior walls, excluding 400 sq. ft. of masonry accents. Demolition of existing wood siding included. Installation of James Hardieplank Select Cedarmill Lap Siding in Iron Gray."*

### 2.2. Clear Project Scope
All work regions are marked on the Digital Twin. Every interface highlights boundaries, penetrations, and edge conditions (e.g., specific window trims, soffits, and fascia zones to be touched or left untouched).

### 2.3. Visual Concepts
Contractors receive high-fidelity, photorealistic AI renderings and Before/After sliding comparisons created by the homeowner. This eliminates aesthetic misunderstandings and aligns both parties on the finished product before anyone touches a hammer.

### 2.4. Budget Expectations
Instead of blind bidding, contractors see the homeowner’s budget range, alongside localized material and labor calculations generated by the AI pricing engine. This ensures budget expectations are aligned before proposal preparation.

### 2.5. Property and Structural Context
Contractors receive a complete profile of the home's active materials, foundation style, roof pitch, wind loads, and thermal zones. This allows them to identify potential structural obstacles (such as access limitations or load requirements) without needing an immediate onsite visit.

### 2.6. Verified Information (Measurements & Quantities)
Measurements are verified through 3D spatial scans and point clouds. Wall heights, plane areas, and trim runs are provided with a calculated accuracy tolerance (e.g., *98.7% Accuracy Tolerance via LiDAR Scan*).

### 2.7. Outstanding Questions Portal
Each opportunity features a dedicated section for clarifying technical questions. Questions and answers are made visible to all matched contractors, establishing a fair, transparent bidding environment and preventing redundant inquiries.

## 3. The Contractor Proposal Workflow

Through the Habitat Contractor Portal, qualified teams can prepare and submit complete proposals in four structured, digital steps:

```
+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                      CONTRACTOR PROPOSAL WORKFLOW                      |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                                        |
|  [ Step 1: Review Opportunity Package ]                                 |
|  Access 3D Twin layers, verified metrics, and past passport logs       |
|                               |                                        |
|                               v                                        |
|  [ Step 2: Formulate Precise Estimates ]                               |
|  Structure labor, materials, and municipal permit line items           |
|                               |                                        |
|                               v                                        |
|  [ Step 3: Attach Digital Contracts & Warranties ]                     |
|  Select liability terms, work timelines, and warranty structures        |
|                               |                                        |
|                               v                                        |
|  [ Step 4: Submit Proposal ]                                           |
|  Proposal goes live in the homeowner's secure comparison grid          |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Step 1: Review Opportunity Package
The contractor logs into their dashboard and reviews their matched opportunities. They use an interactive 3D viewer to explore the home's Digital Twin, toggle structural layers (e.g., framing, framing sheathing, or finish surfaces), and inspect past maintenance logs from the Digital Passport.

### Step 2: Formulate Precise Estimates
Contractors use a guided estimation interface to build out their bids. Because the materials are pre-selected and quantities are verified, they can auto-populate material costs using pre-integrated wholesale supplier networks or input their own rates. They add clear, itemized line items for:
* Demolition & Preparation
* Material Quantities (Skins, Trim, Fasteners, Accessories)
* Specialized Labor Hours
* Local Permitting & Municipal Fees
* Project Contingency reserves

### Step 3: Attach Digital Contracts and Warranties
The contractor sets their proposed timeline (start date, estimated completion date) and links their standard digital contract agreement. They select the exact labor warranties they will provide (e.g., *10-Year Craftsmanship Warranty*) and confirm details of any manufacturer-backed extended warranties.

### Step 4: Submit Proposal
The contractor runs a final validation check to ensure all licensing, insurance, and compliance requirements are met. They click **"Submit Proposal"**, instantly delivering their bid to the homeowner's comparison workspace. No customer phone numbers are revealed during this process, maintaining privacy and preventing unsolicited sales calls.

## 4. Building Trust through Passport Evidence

Contractors do not have to rely on marketing hype or paid reviews to establish credibility. Instead, they can showcase verified experience and build trust using **Passport Evidence**:

* **Historical Work Logs:** When a contractor completes a project on Habitat, the system automatically writes a verified work record to the property's Digital Passport. This becomes an immutable, cryptographically signed reference showing exactly what the contractor built, the materials used, and the final verification report.
* **Automatic Portfolio Verification:** Contractors can showcase completed projects on their public profile, supported by before/after photography, verified invoices, and official inspector sign-offs. This provides prospective clients with authenticated proof of quality, eliminating fake reviews and misleading portfolios.
