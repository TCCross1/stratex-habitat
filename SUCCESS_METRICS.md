## 2. Level 1: Emotional & Engagement Metrics

These metrics evaluate the homeowner's digital relationship with Habitat, tracking sentiment, platform trust, and recurring seasonal engagement.

### 2.1. Net Promoter Score (NPS) & Qualitative Sentiment
* **Definition:** Measures the likelihood of homeowners recommending Habitat to other owners, captured at key positive milestones.
* **UX Strategy:** NPS prompts are explicitly banned during active maintenance tasks or troubleshooting. They are triggered exclusively after major accomplishments (e.g., following a completed renovation project or when awarding a 4-season care streak badge).
* **Target Benchmark:** **NPS > 75** (indicative of deep user trust and organic word-of-mouth growth).

### 2.2. Season-over-Season (SoS) Retention Rate
* **Definition:** The percentage of verified homeowners who log in and interact with their home command center during consecutive climatic seasons (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter).
* **Why it matters:** Unlike standard Daily/Monthly Active Users (DAU/MAU) which promote mindless scrolling, home stewardship operates on seasonal environmental cycles. SoS retention is the true indicator of long-term utility.
* **Calculation:**
  $$\text{SoS Retention} = \frac{\text{Homeowners active in Season } N \cap \text{Active in Season } N-1}{\text{Homeowners active in Season } N-1} \times 100$$
* **Target Benchmark:** **SoS Retention > 88%** after Year 1.

## 3. Level 2: Stewardship & Adherence Metrics

These metrics track physical actions taken to protect the property, measuring how successfully Habitat translates digital insights into real-world maintenance.

### 3.1. The Maintenance Streak Index (MSI)
* **Definition:** The percentage of homeowners who complete 100% of their recommended seasonal preventative tasks (e.g., winterizing spigots, checking smoke alarms, clearing gutters) on time.
* **UX Impact:** Tracks the adoption of proactive care patterns, rewarded through the Celebration Engine with the "Master of Preservation" badge.
* **Target Benchmark:** **MSI > 65%** of active users achieving a perfect 4-season care streak.

### 3.2. Secure Document & Warranty Density
* **Definition:** The average number of certified warranties, appliance receipts, and municipal permits uploaded and anchored to physical assets on the 3D Twin.
* **Why it matters:** A higher document density reduces information asymmetry, instantly raising the property's digital pedigree during an equity valuation.
* **Target Benchmark:** **Average > 6 verified documents** uploaded per property within Year 1.

## 4. Level 3: Financial & Real-World Value Metrics

These metrics prove the ultimate financial value of Habitat, tracking insurance savings, appraisal premiums, and successful Digital Passport transfers.

### 4.1. The Property Equity Premium (PEP)
* **Definition:** The difference in market value appreciation between a Habitat-managed home (with a verified Property Passport) and a comparable local property of similar age and square footage.
* **Data Gathering:** Calculated through partner bank valuation API feeds and comparative market analyses (CMA) during home sales or refinancing.
* **Target Benchmark:** **PEP > 4.5% equity premium** for homes with an unbroken, certified Digital Passport.

### 4.2. Insurance Premium Discount Correlation (IPD)
* **Definition:** The annual financial savings secured by the homeowner on their property insurance policy, driven by verified maintenance and storm-resilience logs.
* **Lobbying Goal:** Partner with national carriers to offer automatic insurance discount tiers (e.g., Bronze, Silver, Gold Shield) verified directly by Stratex Core.
* **Target Benchmark:** **Average 12% reduction** in annual homeowner insurance premiums.

### 4.3. Passport Handover Conversion Rate (PHCR)
* **Definition:** The percentage of property transfers where the seller successfully transfers their unbroken digital twin and historic timeline to the incoming buyer.
* **Why it matters:** The ultimate validation of Habitat as the permanent operating system for homeownership is its survival across property handovers.
* **Target Benchmark:** **PHCR > 92%** of sales involving a Habitat-enabled property.
