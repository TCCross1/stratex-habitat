# ROOF QUESTION EXPERIENCE
## PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE ARCHITECTURE

This document records the four-level progressive response experience implemented for the standard homeowner question: **"Do I need a new roof?"** (Task 4).

---

## 1. THE FOUR PROGRESSIVE LEVELS

The Home Steward response is designed to display information in structured, digestible layers to prevent user cognitive overload while providing absolute truth and traceability.

### LEVEL 1 — DIRECT ANSWER
A clear, calm summary of the current state of the roof. It avoids alarmist phrasing and truthfully defers to unknown factors rather than fabricating certainty.
* **Example:** *"Your current records do not confirm that the roof requires immediate replacement. The latest approved inspection identified the materials as generally sound, while underlayment structural health still needs verification."*

### LEVEL 2 — WHY THIS MATTERS
Explains likely consequences and timing without manufacturing urgency. It frames the condition based on physical rules (e.g., weather cycles) rather than sales timelines.
* **Example:** *"While the outer shingle layer is intact, unresolved thermal inefficiencies on the North slope could accelerate localized shingle wear during summer storm peaks."*

### LEVEL 3 — SUPPORTING INFORMATION
Exposes the analytical data points undergirding the response:
* **Published Explanation:** official Passport assessment.
* **Property Geometry & Material:** 3,200 sqft asphalt shingles installed in 2019 (estimated).
* **Confidence & Assumptions:** High confidence on age/materials, Low confidence on structural underlayment.
* **Unknowns:** Tactile condition of the wood decking.

### LEVEL 4 — TRACE
Provides deep-dive links to authorized external source systems:
* **Passport Link:** Reference ID `#PASS-RF-904` (version 2.1).
* **Timeline Events:** Link to the 2019 installation records and 2026 thermal scan event.

---

## 2. IMMUTABLE ANSWERING RESTRICTIONS

The response engine strictly enforces the following engineering guidelines:
1. **No Fabricated Failures:** Do not declare a roof failed unless there is verified material evidence (e.g., structural collapse or open decking decay).
2. **No Invented Service Life:** Avoid promising an exact remaining service life (e.g., "7.5 years"). State age as estimated and ranges as probabilistic.
3. **No Assimilation of Claims:** Homeowner assertions (e.g., "The storm damaged my roof last week") are recorded as `HOMEOWNER-REPORTED` and are never promoted to `VERIFIED` without professional physical survey certification.
4. **Engineering vs. Planning:** Cost ranges and timelines are explicitly presented as planning estimates, never as certified structural or architectural engineering judgements.
